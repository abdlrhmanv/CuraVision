Brain Tumors: Overview

Gliomas are tumors that originate from glial cells in the brain. 
They represent about 30% of all brain tumors. Common symptoms include headaches, seizures, and cognitive changes.

Meningiomas are typically benign tumors that grow from the meninges, 
the protective layers around the brain. They account for approximately 37% of all brain tumors.

MRI Scans: Magnetic Resonance Imaging is the preferred method for brain tumor diagnosis. 
It provides detailed images of brain structures and can help identify tumor location, size, and characteristics.
